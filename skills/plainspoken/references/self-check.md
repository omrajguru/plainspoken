# Self-check

Run every check before you present a draft as finished, in both the full and the quick flow. Fix what you can first, then report.

## Checks

1. **Point first.** The piece states its subject or result in the first paragraph.
2. **One mode.** The whole piece stays in the mode picked in step 2, or each section is clearly in one mode.
3. **Slop.** Nothing from `references/slop.md` appears, except items the voice file allows. Check captions, headings, summaries, and frontmatter too.
4. **No em dashes,** unless the voice file allows them.
5. **Not over-corrected.** Sentence lengths vary, paragraphs develop a thought, and the author's view is present where the mode allows it.
6. **Clarity.** Every term is explained before it is relied on, every "it" and "this" points at one thing, and each thing has one name throughout.
7. **Voice.** Person, formality, spelling, punctuation, and openings match the voice file. Words the author never uses do not appear.
8. **Numbers.** Every number says how it was measured, or the text says it was not measured.
9. **Approved only.** Every link, name, quote, and sensitive item is on the approved list. No parked point and no banned topic appears.
10. **Headings** say what their section is about, in sentence case.
11. **Metadata matches.** Any title, excerpt, summary, or TL;DR says what the body actually says, and any slug matches the title.
12. **Every paragraph earns its place.** Remove any paragraph the reader would not miss.

## The final reading test

Read the draft once more as the reader, and answer:

- Could someone new to the subject understand it by the end?
- Does the teaching happen without being announced?
- Does it praise the work rather than the writer?
- Does it present conclusions without forcing them on the reader?
- Would the author recognise it as their own writing?

If the prose sounds impressive but the idea has become harder to understand, rewrite it. If it is clear but lifeless, improve the rhythm and vocabulary without making it more complicated.

## Report format

Put the report under the draft:

```
Self-check

| # | Check | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Point first | Pass |
| 3 | Slop | Fixed: removed "seamless" (intro) and a "not just X, but Y" contrast (section 2) |
| 8 | Numbers | Fail: "3x faster" in section 2 has no method. Add how it was measured, or cut it. |
| ... | ... | ... |

Reading test: pass.
```

Report every check, not only the failures. A check you fixed counts as "Fixed", with what you changed.
